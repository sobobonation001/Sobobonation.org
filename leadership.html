Leadership Through Service

SOBOBO NATION promotes leadership that encourages communication, participation, cooperation and community-focused action.

Our Leadership Approach

The leadership structure of SOBOBO NATION is focused on coordination, communication, participation and responsible engagement.

Leadership activities may involve organising programmes, coordinating community engagement, sharing information and creating opportunities for people to participate.

Key Leadership Areas

C

Coordination

Coordinating activities, programmes and community engagement initiatives.

I

Communication

Supporting clear communication and sharing relevant information with members and participants.

P

Participation

Creating opportunities for individuals and groups to participate and contribute.

Leadership Responsibilities

  • Provide direction for the organisation and its activities.
  • Encourage unity, communication and constructive participation.
  • Support the planning and coordination of programmes and events.
  • Promote responsible sharing of information.
  • Encourage volunteers and members to contribute their ideas and skills.
  • Support relationships and collaboration within the community.

Community Leadership

Effective community leadership involves listening, communication, cooperation and responsible participation.

SOBOBO NATION encourages members and participants to contribute positively by sharing ideas, supporting programmes and engaging respectfully with others.

Growing the Leadership Structure

As SOBOBO NATION develops, additional leadership, coordination and volunteer roles may be introduced where necessary to support its activities.

Details of officially established positions and responsibilities can be added to this page as the organisation's structure develops.
