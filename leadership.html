Leadership

Leadership, vision and direction of SOBOBO NATION

Our Leadership

SOBOBO NATION is guided by leadership committed to unity, community participation, progress and positive development.

Chief Dr. Uloko OJIJI

Initiator, Founder & CEO — SOBOBO NATION

Chief Dr. Uloko OJIJI is the initiator, founder and CEO of SOBOBO NATION. His leadership provides direction for the organisation and supports its commitment to unity, progress, community participation and positive engagement.

Leadership Approach

Unity

Encouraging people to work together, build understanding and strengthen community relationships.

Community Participation

Creating opportunities for individuals and communities to participate in activities, programmes and initiatives.

Progress

Supporting constructive ideas, initiatives and activities that contribute to positive community development.

Responsible Leadership

Promoting responsible participation, communication, cooperation and respect among members and stakeholders.

Leadership Responsibilities

Strategic Direction

Providing overall direction and helping to guide the organisation's goals and activities.

Community Engagement

Supporting communication and engagement between SOBOBO NATION and the communities it serves.

Programme Development

Supporting the development of programmes and initiatives that align with the organisation's objectives.

Partnership & Collaboration

Encouraging constructive relationships with members, organisations, volunteers and community stakeholders.

Growing Leadership Structure

As SOBOBO NATION continues to develop, additional leadership roles, coordinators, committees and community representatives can be added to this page as they are officially established.
