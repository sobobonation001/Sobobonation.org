About SOBOBO NATION

SOBOBO NATION provides a platform for information, communication, community engagement and participation.

The platform seeks to connect individuals, groups, supporters and community stakeholders around shared interests and constructive activities.

The organisation was initiated and founded by Chief Dr. Uloko OJIJI, Founder & CEO of SOBOBO NATION.
