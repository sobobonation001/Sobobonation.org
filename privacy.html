Privacy Policy

Information about how information may be handled when you use the SOBOBO NATION website.

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy provides general information about privacy practices for the SOBOBO NATION website. As website features and information systems develop, this policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

1. Introduction

SOBOBO NATION respects the privacy of visitors, members, volunteers, partners and other individuals who interact with this website.

This policy explains the types of information that may be collected and how such information may be used.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available on the website, information may include:

  • Name and contact details submitted through forms.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Location or other information voluntarily provided.
  • Information submitted through membership or volunteer forms.
  • Messages or enquiries sent through available contact channels.

Visitors should only provide information that is necessary for the purpose of the relevant form or enquiry.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided through the website may be used for appropriate organisational purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Processing membership or volunteer requests.
  • Communicating relevant updates.
  • Organising activities and programmes.
  • Improving website services and user experience.

4. Information Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information submitted through the website from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information should not be shared unnecessarily. Information may only be disclosed where reasonably required for legitimate organisational purposes, with appropriate permission, or where required by applicable law.

6. Third-Party Services

Some website functions may use third-party services or platforms in the future, such as hosting, forms, databases, analytics, communication or media services.

Such services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the relevant policies when using those external services.

7. Website Links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. SOBOBO NATION is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security policies of external websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Visitors should not submit personal information on behalf of children unless they are authorised to do so and have the appropriate consent where required.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services, information systems or organisational practices change.

Website Development Notice:
The SOBOBO NATION website is being developed and may introduce additional forms, databases and online services in the future. This policy should be reviewed and updated when those systems are introduced.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information may be handled through the website, please contact SOBOBO NATION through the Contact page.
