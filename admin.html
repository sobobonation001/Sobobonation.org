⚠️ Important Information

The current News, Events, Gallery, Videos, Membership and Volunteer systems use browser localStorage. Records are saved on the device/browser where they were added. They are not yet stored in a central online database. When Supabase is connected later, the system can be upgraded to allow authorised administrators to manage records across different devices.
