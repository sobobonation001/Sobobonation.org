Our Programmes

Activities and initiatives designed to encourage connection, participation, information sharing and community progress.

Building Through Participation

SOBOBO NATION provides opportunities for individuals, groups and community stakeholders to participate in activities that promote unity, communication and progress.

1

Community Engagement

Activities designed to encourage communication, interaction and participation among community members.

2

Information & Awareness

Sharing useful information, announcements and updates relevant to members and the wider community.

3

Community Development

Supporting ideas and activities that can contribute to positive community development.

4

Networking

Creating opportunities for individuals, groups and organisations to connect and build relationships.

5

Ideas & Innovation

Encouraging people to share constructive ideas, solutions and perspectives.

6

Community Support

Encouraging members and volunteers to contribute their time, knowledge and skills where appropriate.

How You Can Participate

  • Attend SOBOBO NATION events and activities.
  • Become a registered member.
  • Volunteer your time and skills.
  • Share useful ideas and information.
  • Support community-focused programmes.
  • Connect with other members and participants.
